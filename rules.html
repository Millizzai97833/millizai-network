Competition Rules

Monthly crypto prizes are not active in this prototype. A competition may start only after dates, eligibility, prize amounts, funding, scoring and winner verification are publicly announced. Scores are currently local and unverified. Bots, modified clients and exploits must not be used in any future competition. Ad views or clicks never earn points. Planned minimum withdrawal request thresholds are 1 DOGE and 0.002 SOL. No withdrawals are currently available.
